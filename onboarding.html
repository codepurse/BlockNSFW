04 / 05 · Desktop Guard

Step 4 of 5: Make BlockNSFW hard to remove.

Anyone can remove or disable this extension from the browser in two clicks. Desktop Guard, a separate app for Windows and Linux now in beta, closes that gap.

  • Remove and Disable are greyed out

    A companion app on your computer turns off the browser’s buttons for this extension.

  • Protection repairs itself

    A background service re-applies protection if anything tampers with it.

  • Uninstalling needs a password

    A parent or an accountability partner holds it, not you.
